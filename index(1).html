FAQ

Why USDT, and which network?

USDT lets students in any country pay without card or banking restrictions. We accept only USDT on the TRON network (TRC20). Sending on another network (ERC20, BEP20…) may result in permanent loss of funds.

How fast do I get access?

After you send your TXID, we confirm the payment on the blockchain and deliver your materials, usually within a few hours.

Do courses include the software?

No. We teach the software; we do not sell or distribute it. You will need your own legal license (most universities and companies provide one).

Can I pre-register for a course marked “Coming soon”?

Yes. Message us and we will notify you at launch with an early-bird discount.

Do you offer team or university licences?

Yes. Contact us for group pricing and corporate training.
